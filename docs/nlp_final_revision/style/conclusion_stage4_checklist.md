## What to check

1. The conclusion does not claim a ready OCR system.
2. The main result is clearly synthetic-to-real gap.
3. The real-OCR negative result is framed as a scientific finding, not failure.
4. Future work mentions verified OCR/reference chunks and page-level split without leakage.
5. Old Tatar/Bashkir are future target sources, while current real benchmark remains Ottoman Turkish.
6. There is no "таким образом" template transition.
